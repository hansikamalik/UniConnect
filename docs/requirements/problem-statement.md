# Problem Statement

Version: 1.0
Date: 16 November 2026
Author: Hansika (Project Lead & Requirements Engineer)

## Problem

University information is spread across notice boards, messaging groups, paper registers and separate offices. Students struggle to find marks, notices, hostel information and mess information in one place. Hostel and mess staff handle complaints and requests informally, with little tracking. The administration has no single source of truth.

## Proposed solution

UniConnect is a centralized web platform that connects students, faculty, hostel wardens, mess administrators and the university administration. It brings academic information, hostel services and mess services into one system with role-based access, replacing scattered notices, paper forms and informal communication.

## Objectives

- Provide one login-based platform for five user roles with clearly separated permissions.
- Give students a reliable, read-only view of their academic, hostel and mess information.
- Let students raise requests, queries and complaints through the system instead of informally.
- Give faculty, wardens and mess administrators tools to manage only their own areas.
- Give the university administration full control over accounts and institutional information.
- Design the system so it can be configured and adapted for other universities.

## Intended users and stakeholders

| Stakeholder | Interest in the system |
|---|---|
| Students | Easy access to marks, notices, schedules, hostel and mess information; a trackable way to raise requests and complaints |
| Faculty | Efficient way to record marks, update academic details and publish class notices |
| Hostel wardens | Organised room allocation, complaint tracking, outpass and form approvals |
| Mess administrators | Menu publishing, feedback collection, complaint handling, and knowing who is opting out of meals |
| University administration | Central control of users, calendar, notices, forms and oversight of all data |
| Other universities (future) | Adopting the product with configuration for their own structure |
