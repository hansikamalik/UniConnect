# Research Notes

Version: 1.0
Date: 16 November 2026
Author: Hansika

## Method

We interviewed 4 students (hostel residents and others) about how they currently get notices, marks, hostel services and mess services, and what problems they face. Names are not recorded. Answers below are combined across students; where only one student said something, it is marked.

## Findings

### Notices and academics
- All students said notices, marks and exam schedules are sent by email.
- Students sometimes miss or find out late about notices, mainly because they do not check email in time.

### Hostel
- There is no app or website to report hostel problems (water, electricity, repairs).
- Students email their problems or go to the concerned person directly.
- Fixing problems sometimes takes a long time, and there is no tracking of status.
- Leave is currently handled by submitting a medical leave application for the days a student was absent. No separate outpass process was described.

### Mess
- The monthly menu is sent on WhatsApp groups.
- The daily menu is written on a whiteboard in the mess.
- Feedback and absence information are shared on the WhatsApp group.

### Biggest problems
- There is no single place to do things, and no one to hear complaints and get them fixed, so things get delayed.

### What students want on one website
- A proper timetable and college notices shown directly (1 student).
- A college map (1 student).
- The website divided into sections such as mess, hostel and academics (1 student).
- Everything connected, so students do not have to go to many different places for medical, medicine, bill reimbursement, attendance, etc. (1 student).
- If a student goes home for a few days, the hostel in/out and mess subscription should be paused together, instead of sending a separate email for each (1 student).

## Summary

Main problems found:
1. Information is scattered across email, WhatsApp groups and a whiteboard, and students miss things.
2. No system to report hostel problems or track whether they are fixed.
3. No single place or person to take complaints, so things get delayed.

Anything that changes or adds to our requirements:
- Complaint tracking with status (Pending, In Progress, Resolved) is confirmed as a real need.
- Mess menu and noticeboard are confirmed as real needs.
- Leave/outpass was described only as medical leave applications, so its priority is lower than assumed.
- Linking hostel leave to mess opt-out was requested by a student. This is open question 7 in the team document and should be considered for requirements.
- Timetable display and a college map were suggested. Not in current scope; to be considered as future items.
- Medical, bill reimbursement and attendance integration is outside the current scope.

## Limitations

Only students were interviewed. Staff processes (warden, mess, office) are described from the student side and are not confirmed with staff.
